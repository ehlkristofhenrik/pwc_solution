**Fictive Inc. Employee Policy & Code of Conduct**

**Employment Philosophy & Conduct**
Fictive Inc. thrives on mutual trust, high performance, and respect. We expect every employee to act professionally, foster an inclusive environment, and represent the company's core values in all work-related settings.

---

**Workplace Policies & Benefits**

| Category | Policy Summary |
| --- | --- |
| **Work Arrangement** | Flexible hybrid structure. Employees choose their primary work location, with core sync hours set from **10:00 AM – 3:00 PM EST**. |
| **Time Off (PTO)** | Open PTO policy (minimum 15 days encouraged annually) plus 11 paid company holidays. PTO must be requested 2 weeks in advance for absences over 3 consecutive days. |
| **Sick & Mental Health Leave** | 10 dedicated paid days per year for personal illness, family care, or mental health rest. |
| **Parental Leave** | 16 weeks fully paid parental leave for all primary and secondary caregivers following birth, adoption, or foster placement. |
| **Equipment & Stipend** | Company laptop provided. Employees receive a **$500 one-time home office setup stipend** and a **$75/month remote work allowance**. |

---

**Workplace Conduct & Compliance**

* **Anti-Harassment & Non-Discrimination:** Equal opportunity employer. Zero tolerance for discrimination, harassment, or retaliation based on race, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age, or religion.
* **Acceptable Use of Assets:** Company-provided hardware and software are for business operations. Occasional, reasonable personal use is permitted if it does not violate security guidelines or impair performance.
* **Conflict of Interest:** Employees must disclose any outside employment, freelancing, advisory roles, or financial interests in competitors/vendors to HR prior to engagement.
* **Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure:** Company intellectual property, trade secrets, financial data, and customer information remain strictly confidential during and after employment.

---

**Performance & Discipline**

* **Feedback Culture:** Formal performance check-ins occur bi-annually, complemented by continuous 1-on-1 feedback.
* **Progressive Discipline:** Issues regarding performance or conduct are addressed transparently. The corrective action process generally follows: *Verbal Warning → Written Improvement Plan (PIP) → Termination*. Fictive Inc. reserves the right to terminate employment immediately for severe misconduct (e.g., theft, gross negligence, harassment).
