About US:

Fictive Inc. is the world's largest supplier of sonic screwdrivers, toothpaste, printers and barrels for oil. It was founded by Sir. Robert Fictive, in 1908 and is today among the top 3 fortune 500 companies. The company in 2025 had an estimated 4.7 trillion USD revenue and it operates in 194 countries worldwide. The company has 5000 employees across 7 different nations. The Fictive Charity Foundation is the largest donors on the Middle East, helping poor neightbourhoods get fresh water.
