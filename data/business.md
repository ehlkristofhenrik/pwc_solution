**Fictive Inc. Business Guidelines**

**Core Mission & Standards**
Fictive Inc. delivers high-impact, scalable solutions while maintaining operational integrity. Every employee is expected to align daily actions with three foundational pillars: **Radical Transparency**, **Data-Backed Execution**, and **Customer-First Innovation**.

**Workplace & Conduct**

* **Communication:** Use official channels (Slack, Email) for project alignment. Maintain concise, respectful, and transparent communication across all teams.
* **Remote & Hybrid Work:** Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM EST. Team members are trusted to manage their output asynchronously outside these hours.
* **Ethics & Compliance:** Zero tolerance for conflict of interest, data misuse, or harassment. Raise concerns directly to HR or via the anonymous compliance portal.

**Project & Resource Management**

* **Decision-Making:** Decisions exceeding $5,000 or altering project scope require a written RFC (Request for Comments) document and sign-off from the department head.
* **Data Security:** All company devices must run approved encryption software. Customer PII (Personally Identifiable Information) must never be stored on local drives.
* **Expense Policy:** Standard business travel, software subscriptions, and client meals require prior manager approval. Receipts must be logged within 14 business days.

**Professional Growth & Reviews**

* **Performance Evaluations:** Bi-annual reviews focus on outcomes, peer feedback, and alignment with company goals.
* **Learning Stipend:** Every full-time employee receives a $1,500 annual budget for courses, books, and industry conferences.
