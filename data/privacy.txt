**Fictive Inc. Global Privacy Policy**

**Effective Date:** August 25, 2026

**Last Updated:** August 25, 2026

Fictive Inc. ("we," "our," or "us") values your trust and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and share information when you interact with our websites, platforms, software, and services (collectively, the "Services").

---

**1. Information We Collect**

We collect data to provide, optimize, and secure our Services. This includes:

* **Directly Provided Information:** Account registration details (name, work email, job title, company name), billing/payment credentials, support inquiries, and explicit inputs provided to our interactive tools.
* **Automatically Collected Data:** Technical information gathered via logs and essential storage tokens, including IP address, browser type, operating system, network routing data, and system diagnostics.
* **System & Service Content:** Customer data uploaded into our production environments, subject to strict role-based access control and confidentiality terms.

---

**2. How We Use Information**

| Purpose | Legal Basis (GDPR/Global Frameworks) | Data Categories Used |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Service Operations** | Performance of Contract | Account Data, Technical Data |
| **Billing & Administration** | Legal Obligation / Contract | Payment Credentials, Company Details |
| **Security & Threat Prevention** | Legitimate Interest | IP Address, System Logs, Audit Logs |
| **Product Improvements** | Legitimate Interest / User Consent | Aggregated Usage Metrics, Telemetry |

*Note: Fictive Inc. does not sell, rent, or monetize personal data, nor do we process personal data for cross-context behavioral advertising.*

---

**3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Vendors**

We share data only with trusted vendors and sub-processors operating under strict contractual privacy obligations:

* **Infrastructure & Hosting:** AWS, Google Cloud Platform (Enforcing AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit).
* **Identity & Authentication:** Okta, Auth0 (Enforcing Multi-Factor Authentication).
* **Analytics & Telemetry:** Privacy-compliant, cookieless telemetry engines focused on operational metrics rather than individual profiling.
* **Legal Compliance:** We may disclose information if required by a binding legal subpoena, court order, or governmental audit.

---

**4. Data Retention & Security**

* **Storage Minimization:** We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the operational purposes for which it was collected, or to comply with statutory legal and tax obligations.
* **Security Controls:** Production environments are isolated behind Web Application Firewalls (WAF). Data handling follows zero-trust network principles, and employees undergo background checks and mandatory privacy training.

---

**5. Your Rights & Data Requests**

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, or UK DPA), you have the right to:

* **Access & Portability:** Request a structured copy of the personal data we hold about you.
* **Correction & Deletion:** Request correction of inaccurate information or permanent deletion of your account data.
* **Opt-Out & Preference Signals:** Submit automated opt-out preference signals (such as Global Privacy Control) or withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Data Protection Office at **privacy@fictive.com**. Requests are verified and processed within 30 business days.
